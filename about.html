About Everest Dental

Your Dental Partner in Birgunj. Committed to your smile with world-class facilities and expert care.

Welcome to Everest Dental Service Center

Established in 2060 BS, Everest Dental Service Center has been a trusted name in Birgunj for comprehensive dental care. We combine modern technology with a gentle, patient-first approach to ensure every visit is comfortable and effective.

Our clinic is led by Dr. Manoj Kr. Upadhyay, a highly respected Endodontist specializing in complex root canal treatments, dental trauma, and restorative care. Under his guidance, we have grown into a full-service dental center equipped with advanced digital treatment rooms and in-house pharmacy facilities.

"We believe that a healthy smile is an important part of overall well-being. Our goal is to provide ethical, transparent, and gentle care to every patient who walks through our door."

Whether you need a routine check-up, cosmetic enhancement, or emergency treatment, our team is here to help you achieve and maintain optimal oral health.

Dr. Manoj Kr. Upadhyay

B.S.C., B.D.S., M.D.S. — Director
