About Everest Dental

Your Dental Partner in Birgunj. Committed to your smile 24/7 with world-class facilities and expert care.

Our story

Everest Dental Service Center Pvt. Ltd. is not just a clinic; it's a commitment to healthy smiles in Birgunj. We started with a simple idea: everyone deserves world-class dental care without fear or high bills. Today, we are proud to be recognized as the best in Birgunj for our strict hygiene practices, advanced technology, and patient-first approach.

We focus on prevention first. A regular check-up costs far less than a painful emergency, and we are happy to teach you how to keep your teeth strong at home. From the moment you walk in, our goal is to make you feel comfortable, informed, and cared for.

No rushed appointments. No hidden costs. Just honest dentistry backed by modern infrastructure.

Our promise

  • We quote the price upfront.

What we stand for

Three values guide everything we do — from the front desk to the dental chair.
