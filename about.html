About Everest Dental

Committed to your smile 24/7. Caring for your teeth is our whole job — and we take it seriously.

Our story

Everest Dental started with a simple idea: everyone deserves good dental care, without fear or high bills. What began as a small one-chair clinic is now a trusted neighbourhood practice serving families across Darbhanga.

We focus on prevention first. A regular check-up costs far less than a painful emergency, and we are happy to teach you how to keep your teeth strong at home.

From the moment you walk in, our goal is to make you feel comfortable, informed, and cared for. No rushed appointments. No hidden costs. Just honest dentistry.

Our promise

  • We explain before we treat.
  • We quote the price upfront.
  • We never rush an appointment.
  • We follow up after every treatment.
  • We treat every patient like family.

What we stand for

Three values guide everything we do — from the front desk to the dental chair.

Honesty

Clear explanations and upfront pricing. You will never be surprised by a bill.

Gentleness

We work at your pace. Nervous patients are always welcome here.

Prevention

We would rather help you avoid problems than fix them later.

Accessibility

Fair prices and flexible hours so care is within everyone's reach.
